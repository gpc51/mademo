# Made | Plug and Play Pitch

Duración objetivo: 2 minutos 35 segundos a 2 minutos 40 segundos.

## English

### 01 / Cover, 0:00–0:12
Made is predictive AI for factories and fleets. We turn physical signals into actions that prevent unplanned downtime.

### 02 / Problem, 0:12–0:29
Downtime begins before the visible failure. Signals sit across separate systems, lead time disappears, and production, quality and delivery decline together. A high-severity event can cost USD 150K. Industrial operations average 326 hours of unplanned downtime a year.

### 03 / Product, 0:29–0:48
Made connects to the signals already available in PLCs, SCADA and vehicle networks. When signals are missing, we add them through IO-Link. The edge processes locally, sends secure read-only events, and delivers the next action with context and risk. This is the decision layer operators use before a stop becomes expensive.

### 04 / Customer Case, 0:48–1:10
At ArtisanLabs, a recurring CNC cutting-tool failure stopped production three to four times per machine per year. Made combined feed rate and operating hours, estimated remaining useful life, and prescribed maintenance with an inventory review. Over six months, the line had one unplanned stop versus 1.5 to two expected. That is a 43% reduction at the historical midpoint. The model is USD 4K upfront and USD 1.5K per production line per month.

### 05 / Revenue Model, 1:10–1:28
We validate value through pilots, expand across lines, assets, sites and fleets, and then distribute natively through OEM ecosystems. The near-term pilot and first-cohort value is USD 387K. The existing expansion pipeline represents more than USD 34M. The long-term opportunity is embedding the intelligence layer into industrial hardware ecosystems.

### 06 / Competitive Advantage, 1:28–1:43
Telematics providers show vehicle visibility. Predictive platforms identify failures. Made turns signal and context into a prescribed action across physical operations. Our next layer, industrial SLMs and Made Edge, compounds that advantage from every deployed asset.

### 07 / Team, 1:43–1:56
We combine industrial access, edge deployment and applied AI. Gerardo leads product and commercial strategy. Ramiro leads edge, data and deployments. Our team brings PhD-level machine learning and computer-vision expertise to the operating edge.

### 08 / Current Status, 1:56–2:13
We were accelerated by Startup Chile, launched Made OS, validated with an active design partner and Founders Inc. Canopy, and became a BHP finalist for a mine pilot. We have raised USD 125K to date. The company is now entering the deployment phase.

### 09 / The Ask, 2:13–2:40
We are raising USD 1.8M for 18 months of repeatable industrial deployment. Capital funds product and deployment engineering, field operations, enterprise sales, and security. This round targets three pilot-to-contract conversions, more than 100 live lines and assets, and USD 1M in contracted ARR.
