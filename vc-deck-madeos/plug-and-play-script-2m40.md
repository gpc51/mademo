# Made | Plug and Play Pitch

Target duration: 2 minutes 35 seconds to 2 minutes 40 seconds.

## English

### 01 / Cover, 0:00-0:10
Made is predictive AI for factories and fleets. We turn physical signals into operational actions that prevent unplanned downtime.

### 02 / Problem, 0:10-0:26
Most operations see the failure only when it is already expensive. Signals are fragmented across PLCs, SCADA and vehicle systems; the expert who knows what matters is often the bottleneck. A high-severity event can cost USD 150K, and industrial operations lose an estimated 326 hours a year to unplanned downtime.

### 03 / Product, 0:26-0:45
Made connects existing machine and vehicle signals. When the data is incomplete, we add IO-Link instrumentation at the edge. We process the telemetry, add operating context and deliver one clear prescription: what to do, who owns it and when. Our models learn from technician interventions and operator feedback, turning tacit operating experience into a reusable decision layer.

### 04 / Customer Case, 0:45-1:03
Let me make that concrete with a real case. At a furniture manufacturing plant in Mexico, Made helped reduce monthly unplanned stops by 50 to 67 percent. The plant had more than USD 12K in monthly loss exposure. CNC cutting-tool failures had caused two to three unplanned stops per machine per month. Made combined feed rate and operating hours, estimated remaining useful life, and scheduled maintenance with an inventory check.

### 05 / Revenue Model, 1:03-1:22
Our business model starts with paid entry pilots. Deployment is a one-time fee: USD 4K for industrial units or USD 1K through an API connection. We then earn recurring baseline software revenue of USD 1.5K per line or USD 495 per vehicle per month, plus a 10 percent performance fee on verified value created.

We enter large accounts through paid pilots and expand from there by asset, site and fleet. Our qualified expansion path is more than USD 34M. Over time, we aim to embed Made as a native operating layer within PLC, controller and vehicle OEM ecosystems.

### 06 / Competitive Advantage, 1:22-1:36
Telematics creates visibility. Predictive tools identify anomalies. Made owns the operational decision: signal, context, confidence and prescribed action across factories and fleets. Every deployment strengthens the data foundation for our industrial SLMs and Made Edge.

### 07 / Team, 1:36-1:48
I bring leadership, manufacturing and industrial design experience to Made. Ramiro leads technology development, data infrastructure and on-site deployment. We are building Made with PhD-level expertise focused on pushing applied physical AI forward.

### 08 / Current Status, 1:48-2:04
Made launched in January 2026, has been accelerated by Startup Chile and Founders Inc. Canopy, and validated through our first design-partner deployment.
We are a top-three global finalist for a mine pilot at BHP Escondida and a finalist in the CEMEX Ventures Construction Startup Competition.

We have raised USD 125K to date.

### 09 / The Ask, 2:04-2:38

We are raising USD 1.8M to turn controlled deployments into a repeatable operating model.
Our milestones are three pilot-to-contract conversions, more than 100 live lines and assets, and USD 2M in contracted ARR. The purpose of this round is to prove repeatability, then unlock the expansion path already in front of us.
