Unit economics

Software margins. Hardware is a one-time toll.

Modeled from current design-partner deployments and live proposal pricing. To be validated through the first paid conversions.

Edge hardware · one-time
50–75%
$5K gateway fee per asset vs. $1.1K–$2.5K landed cost: Jetson module, 2–4 industrial sensors, field install. Under 10% of Year 1 contract value. Never recurring.
Annual license · recurring
≈85%
Cloud + edge inference runs $5K–$7K per facility per year against a $160K–$480K license, support allocation included. SaaS-grade margin on the recurring layer.
Pilot · 90 days
≈80%
$40K–$90K pilot vs. ~$7K–$12K hard cost: hardware, 3 months of cloud, field travel. The pilot funds its own deployment, and 100% credits to Year 1 on conversion.
Why this stays software-shaped at scale: hardware and install are one-time, charged at margin, and amortize to noise. The recurring layer is inference and per-asset models, not field labor. Enterprise API tier is deliberately unpriced: first 10 integrations free as an acquisition motion, 3 already in evaluation.

Assumptions: one gateway per asset · 2–4 sensors per asset at $200–$400 each · cloud $400–$600/mo per facility · founder field time excluded from pilot COGS.
