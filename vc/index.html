13 / moat and risks14

The moat is the operational learning loop.
The risks are real.

Potential moat

  • Asset-specific operating context
  • Failure-to-action-to-result history
  • Cross-asset operational digital twin
  • Integration into maintenance and dispatch
  • Domain-specific prescriptions

Risks and mitigations

  • Incumbents already offer predictive maintenance: prove incremental value
  • OEM data access varies: integrate with existing telematics first
  • Safety and liability: read-only, no safety-critical actuation
  • Hardware can erode margins: separate deployment economics
  • Focus may fragment: separate plant and fleet packaging
Market maturity makes honest risk disclosure part of the pitch14 / 16
